🌅 Morning Checks
Check soil moisture at root depth Water
Finger 2–3" in. Tomatoes/peppers: 12–18" zone. Strawberries: 6–8". Water if dry at depth.
Check rain log — did it rain recently? Water
½"+ in last 24 hrs → skip watering. ¼–½" → check soil first. Log any overnight rain.
Water if needed Water
Base only, deep soak. Tomatoes: 1–1.5" per week. Cucumbers/watermelons: consistent moisture. Avoid wet foliage.
Walk & inspect all plants Check
Look for wilt, yellowing, purple stems, curling, spots, drooping. Early detection = easy fix.
Check undersides of leaves for aphids/eggs Pest
Ant trails on stems = aphids nearby. Look for sticky honeydew, distorted new growth.
☀️ Midday Tasks
Tomato suckers — remove below 1st flower cluster Plant
Suckers grow in the V between main stem and branch. Snap small ones off cleanly.
Train cucumber & watermelon vines Plant
Guide daily — vines can move 6–12" per day in peak season. Secure loosely.
Check pepper branching — hold fruiting until 12"+ Plant
Pinch first buds if plant is small. More fruit later from a stronger plant.
Check strawberry runners & fruit Plant
Pin runners if propagating, cut if not needed. Pick ripe fruit promptly.
